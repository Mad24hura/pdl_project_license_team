Frequently Asked Questions

❓ How to request a new license?

To request a new license, navigate to the "Request New License" section in your dashboard and fill out the required information including software name and justification.

❓ How to renew an expiring license?

Contact your system administrator to renew an expiring license. You can also submit a support request through this help section.

❓ What if my license request is rejected?

If your license request is rejected, you can submit a new request with additional justification or contact support for more information about the rejection.

❓ How can I check which licenses are installed on my device?

Use the "Search Available Licenses for a Device" feature in your dashboard to view all licenses assigned to your device.

❓ What happens when a license expires?

When a license expires, you will lose access to the software. It's important to renew licenses before they expire to avoid service interruption.
